How we work

Small team.
Short feedback loops.

Design, code, art, and UI stay close together. That makes it easier to test the core idea early and keep the finished game focused.

01

Game design

We prototype the core loop first, then build levels and progression around what already feels good to play.

02

Unity engineering

03
